    How to play

    Goal

    Knock everyone off the floating island. Last one standing wins the round. Hits only daze, so grab & throw buddies over the fence or into the river. The island starts shrinking after 5 minutes.

    Controls
    Drag anywhere to move JUMP button PUNCH: hit or use your weapon Hold GRAB, let go to throw THROW your weapon: tap to throw at the nearest buddy, or drag to aim Tap a weapon icon (top right) to pick it up or swap · 🥊 gloves KO in one punch Hits only daze! Grab & throw buddies over the fence or into the river 🎥 Follow cam: drag the right side to look around
    Modes

    Free-for-all: everyone for themselves. Teams: last team standing wins, and friendly fire is ON.

    Classic: play round after round, points keep adding up. First to N: teams stay the same and rounds keep going until a player or team reaches the target score and wins the match.
